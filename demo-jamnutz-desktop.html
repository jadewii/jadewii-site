JAMNUTZ is the non-DAW for jammers: a Mac JUCE app, ten tracks of hosted AU instruments, played by a hardware box or the computer keyboard, generative sequencers, no timeline. Slots 1-8 are synths, slot 9 is the drum machine, slot 10 is the sampler. CC 70-77 and CC 16-23 both turn a track's first eight parameters, the same ccToKnob() math as the strip above, and CC 7 is always the channel volume. Every other MIDI input is live, including ones plugged in after launch.

Only AU gets scanned on a Mac, since every plugin ships one and the VST3 copies just add duplicates plus the odd plugin that throws inside dlopen. Scanning still wraps each file in a try/catch, a plugin that throws during load gets skipped and logged as "a plugin threw during load" instead of taking the scan down with it. AKANE is one of the 223, and it sits on its own blocklist.txt line because it crashes inside its own processBlock when hosted here.
