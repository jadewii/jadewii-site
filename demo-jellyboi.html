JellyBoi is the flagship, a pastel mountain kalimba with crown modes for play, sound, scale and arp, a face-tap looper with auto-close and an overdub ring, and blue step record. It runs 16 sounds and 16 scales with octave swipes and a hold-to-delete pie, and it sleeps into always-on display instead of quitting.

The runtime rule is what makes it survive the wrist going down: an extended-runtime session grants about an hour of background time per start, and it restarts on every foreground activation so the window resets each time the watch is raised. Sessions can only start while the app is active, so ensureRunning() reactivates the audio engine on the same interruption path, foregrounding included.

Step record is quantized: notes land in order, one per step, at an eighth note each, and the 16th step starts playback. Everything above is the same case captured on the watchOS simulator, cycling between the still frame and the recorded video.
