Apps, the watch fleet and the two on the App Store

SwiftUI · AVAudioEngine · watchOS and iOS · sixteen real simulator captures, tap a name

Seventeen watch apps live in one folder, every one with its own PROJECT.md, and sixteen of them build to the simulator and were captured on 2026-09-06. JellyBoi is the flagship, a kalimba that keeps its audio alive when the wrist drops. NoiseFace and WaveFace are on the App Store. TODOMAI is a todo app that talks instead of buzzing. Each one taught the same lesson about the audio session, the runtime session and the lock screen.

NoiseFace is a 12-color noise generator with modulation, presets and real-time control, 56 commits, shipped for iOS and tvOS. WaveFace is a synth with a face that reads its own oscillator, v1.1 on the store with StoreKit 2. TODOMAI is watchOS task management with voice input, and it speaks through AVSpeechSynthesizer. The watch fleet runs from 220 lines (SkyBoi) to 2,855 (Picnic, the engine pattern the others copy).
