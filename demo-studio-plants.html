Studio Plants

generated art · CSS sway · Web Audio beat detection · modularmusician.com

modularmusician.com is a free network for people who make modular music. Every member gets a plant for their studio. The plants dance when music plays on the site. That is the whole feature, and it took three separate pieces of work to make it feel right.

1. The art

136 plants, generated in my own image studio in one consistent style: same lighting, same pot proportions, transparent background, drawn so the pot base sits on a flat line. Consistency was the hard part. A model will happily give you 136 beautiful plants that do not belong on the same shelf. The style and the framing were locked first, then the batch was run, then the ones that broke the rules were thrown out. Three extra images cover growth: a seed, a sprout, and a sapling.

2. The dance

Every plant on the site is one image. It sways from the base of the pot, like a real plant in a breeze, and nothing else moves. No cutting the image into layers, no bending, no scale pulse. That rule came from trying the fancier versions and hating them.

The sway is a sine wave on a site clock, with a per-plant phase so eight plants on a shelf never move in unison, scaled by the beat. All of it is CSS. The JavaScript only writes three numbers onto the page: the clock, the beat energy, and whether music is on.

.dp { --s: calc(sin((var(--t) * (0.9 + var(--i) * 0.05) + var(--i) * 1.7) * 1rad)
            * var(--amp) * 1.25 * (0.55 + 0.45 * var(--beat)) * var(--on)); }
.dp .dp-img { transform-origin: 50% 97%; transform: rotate(calc(var(--s) * 1deg)); }

3. The beat

An analyser taps whatever audio element is playing and reads the low band. It is smoothed so a quiet ambient track dances as hard as a techno one, and floored so hiss does not count. On iOS the audio element is never routed through Web Audio, because Safari suspends the context on screen lock and the music dies with it. The analyser listens to a copy of the stream instead. That one line cost a day.

No music means no motion. The shelf on this page runs a synthetic beat so you can see it without playing anything.

What it is doing on a portfolio for AI work

Because it is generated art in production, not a gallery. The model did the drawing. The judgment about what to keep, the rules that made 136 images read as one set, and the engineering that made them feel alive came from a person. That split is the job.
