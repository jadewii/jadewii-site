modularmusician.com is a free network for people who make modular music. Every member gets a plant for their studio, picked by a hash of their name, and the plants dance when music plays on the site. Each plant is one image that sways from the base of the pot. The sway is a sine on the site clock with a per-plant phase, scaled by the beat, all in CSS.

The plants were generated in my own image studio in one consistent style: same lighting, same pot proportions, transparent background, the pot base on a flat line. The style and the framing were locked first, then the batch was run, then the ones that broke the rules were thrown out. Consistency was the hard part. A model will happily give you 135 beautiful plants that do not belong on the same shelf.

An analyser taps whatever audio element is playing and reads the low band, with auto-gain against the loudest level heard recently so a quiet ambient track dances as hard as a techno one, and a floor so hiss does not count. On iOS the audio element is never routed through Web Audio, because Safari suspends the context on screen lock and the music dies with it. The analyser listens to a copy of the stream through captureStream() instead. That one line cost a day. The shelf here runs the site's synthetic beat so it moves without playing anything.
