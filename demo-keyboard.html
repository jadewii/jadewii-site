Play it, every note is in key

The circle keyboard from an early JAMNUTZ homepage, ported verbatim: outlined circles for the white keys, filled for the black, a triangle-wave voice. The new part is the lock: pick a key and a scale, and every touch snaps into it, so there is no wrong note.

🔒 IN KEY

Click or tap the circles. Or use your computer row A S D F G H J K L. Hold for sustain.

Where it comes from: an early JAMNUTZ homepage, one 1,416-line file called jamnutz_homepage_v2_one_interactive.html, whose title reads "a new way to make music on the computer". The pitch math (line 471), the triangle oscillator with the comment "Okinori's signature sound" (line 485) and the circle layout, each key placed at (slot + 0.5) over the white-key count (line 606), are copied from it unchanged. The in-key lock is the only new code on this page.
