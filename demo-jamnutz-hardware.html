Before September 6 slot 0 (the drum channel) played every incoming note through the melodic tom voice. Now GM_DRUM_LANE reads the note number and routes it to one of eight canonical lanes, kick, snare, low tom, high tom, clap, cowbell and two hats, with a chromatic fallback from C1 for a controller that isn't sending the GM layout. CCs work the same way: ranges 70-77 and 16-23 both hit the same eight macro knobs so most boxes work without any setup, a handful of named CCs (74 cutoff, 71 resonance, 73 attack, 75 decay, 72 release) grab their specific knob instead of a numbered slot, and CC 7 is always the slot's volume.

Web MIDI itself needs Chrome, Edge or Opera, Firefox 108 or newer, or Safari 18+ on macOS or iPadOS, over HTTPS. The same map now also shows up as a MIDI pill in the strip on jamnutz.com/standalone, wiring up on its own when the browser already granted permission.
