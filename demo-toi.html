TOI semis is the semimodular row inside Eurorack Grid. Each panel runs a real Cmajor plugin voice compiled to a WASM audio worklet, one long faceplate sized to fill an 84HP row exactly. This is the JW-CZ panel below, its real 26 params and 15 jacks, drawn with the plugin kit's own metal knob traced from the faceplate code. Drag a knob and it only moves the value shown here, no audio is wired up on this page.

The panel comes from a generator, not hand art. scripts/gen-toi.mjs runs each tribute patch through Cmajor's own compiler and reads back its real endpoint list, then bundles it into one of 66 separate files under case/toi-*.gen.js. The ids on every knob above match those Cmajor endpoints one to one, so whatever a knob sends goes straight into the running patch once the worklet is live.

Until that worklet loads, the OUT jack just passes silence through a plain gain node, so patching never has to wait on the DSP. The knob itself is the plugin kit's own StyledKnob::Metal, traced 1:1 from the faceplate code, not a redraw.
