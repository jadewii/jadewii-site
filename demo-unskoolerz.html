A kid can ask the tutor anything while they're studying a card. The key for that lives on a Cloudflare Worker, not in the browser, because anything shipped in the app's own JavaScript can be read by whoever opens the page (tutor-worker/src/index.js:3-5). The rule I care about most in that system prompt: never invent a fact. 
HOW TO ANSWER
- Answer the actual question, directly, in 2 to 5 sentences. No preamble, no "great question", no restating the question back.
- Plain, concrete language. Short sentences. Real numbers, real names, real places. Never baby-talk, never emoji, never exclamation marks.
- Say true things. If experts disagree, say they disagree. If nobody knows, say "nobody knows yet" and say what would settle it. Never invent a fact, a date, a name, or a number to fill a gap.
New cards don't go straight into the app either. A nightly run starts under launchd at 3:00 AM (factory/run-nightly.sh:2), works one item off a queue, and writes what it makes into factory/pending/, never into app/src or curriculum directly (factory/NIGHTLY-PROMPT.md:9). I read what's in pending and decide what ships. deploy.sh runs the test suite first, then builds, then deploys, then checks that unskoolerz.pages.dev is actually serving the JS bundle that build produced, not just a 200 status.
