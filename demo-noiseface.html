12 noise colors, real per-sample math, ported from the app · App Store id 6754266069

NoiseFace is a 12-color noise generator, iOS built with SwiftUI and AVAudioEngine. Each button here runs the exact per-sample generator from the app: pink is Voss-McCartney, brown is a leaky integrator with the same 0.02 step and 0.998 decay, and the rest match one case at a time out of the same switch statement. Tap a color, tap another to switch, STOP to kill it.

The app is a 12-color noise generator with modulation, presets, and real-time control, per its own project overview. The colors aren't a filter over white noise, each one is its own generator: Voss-McCartney pink, a random-walk brown, a differentiated blue, a second-order differentiated violet, an A-weighting approximation for gray, a 500Hz-weighted green, an averaged orange, a Box-Muller gaussian for turquoise, and true silence for black.

The UI itself follows a fixed 18pt spacing rule between grid buttons, the same discipline as the audio: nothing is eyeballed, the constants are in the code.
