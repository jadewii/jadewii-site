WaveFace is a powerful yet simple waveform generator for iOS, built to make reference tones and test audio equipment. Every button here runs the app's actual createBuffer math: it counts how many full cycles of your frequency fit in a 0.2 second window, floors that to a whole number, then recomputes the frequency so the buffer's start and end line up exactly. That's why it can loop forever with no click.

The free tier ships 4 waveforms, sine, square, sawtooth and triangle. A one-time purchase unlocks the rest, over 20 in total including noise colors, FM, ring mod and formant shapes, gated by a StoreKit product with id com.waveface.app.premium.

The loop trick is the same for every shape: cyclesInBuffer = floor(frequency * duration), then adjustedFrequency = cyclesInBuffer / duration. The waveform you hear is at the adjusted frequency, not your exact slider value, so the buffer can repeat without a seam. The canvas above marks where the buffer wraps.
