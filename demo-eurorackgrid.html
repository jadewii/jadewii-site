Eurorack Grid, the module roster

browser modular · vanilla JS + Web Audio · live at eurorackgrid.pages.dev

Eurorack Grid is a browser Eurorack rack: pick a case, fill it with modules, patch it, jam, no install. The map above is the real roster, every module code and every brand color pulled straight from the app's own source, not redrawn for this page. Click a brand in the legend and its modules light up, the rest dim.

ROSTER and BRAND_INFO are both hand-maintained arrays at the top of case/app.js, comments and all: the roster is grouped by brand right there in the source, one comment per brand run. app.js is 7,510 lines on its own. shared/engine.js underneath it runs another 5,165.

An earlier code-level audit instantiated all 416 modules the catalog can build, one brand variant at a time, against a mock AudioContext, and found zero dead jacks: every panel jack maps to a live audio node (case/AUDIT.md:5,8). Every knob on a panel doubles as a jack too, drop a CV cable on one and it patches straight into that param (app.js:148, "EVERY KNOB IS A JACK"). And a rack is never silent by accident: if a random build somehow lands with nothing audible, a last-resort function drops a pad or oscillator straight to the output bus (app.js:5988-5990, guaranteeAudible).
